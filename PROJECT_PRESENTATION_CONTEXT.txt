SECTION B: PERSON 2 — ADMIN / MANAGING DIRECTOR OPERATIONS CONSOLE
================================================================================
(Speaker: Operations Director / Technical Lead)

SLIDE 6: Admin Security, Authentication & Complete Page Isolation
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Goal: Provide a dedicated, distraction-free operations console for management.
• Admin Access & Security Features:
  1. Dedicated Admin Tab in Login Modal: Enter authorized admin credentials 
     (credentials hidden from public UI).
  2. Strict Console Isolation:
     - Once logged in as Admin, the system never displays the public landing page 
       or customer showcase.
     - Clicking the company logo or browser navigation stays strictly inside the 
       management pipeline.
     - Only clicking "Logout" terminates the session and returns to the landing page.
  3. Mobile Step-Back Interception: Browser hardware back button closes modals or 
     drawers first and will never accidentally navigate the admin outside the console.

SLIDE 7: High-Level KPI Command Center (Clickable Metrics)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Goal: Real-time pulse of inquiries, ongoing builds, and delivered homes.
• 6 Clickable KPI Metric Cards:
  1. 📥 Total Inquiries: Full count of all customer registrations.
  2. ⏳ Pending Call: Requests requiring manual phone call & payment verification.
  3. 🏗️ Needs to Start: Approved inquiries where drawings/excavation are preparing.
  4. ⚙️ Processing: Active construction under ongoing engineering execution.
  5. 🏆 Finished Units: 100% completed units handed over to buyers.
  6. ❌ Rejected Inquiries: Inquiries declined or cancelled due to budget mismatch.
• Interactive Feature: Clicking ANY metric card directly filters the requests list 
  to that exact stage and highlights the matching on-page tab pill!

SLIDE 8: Admin Request Pipeline & Verification Workflow
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Goal: Execute the "Call and verify payment manual" process effortlessly.
• Each Request Row Displays:
  - Client Full Name, Contact Phone, and Email.
  - Chosen Category, BHK Layout, Target Quotation, and Approx Built-Up Sq.Ft.
  - Submission Date and Timestamp.
  - Prominent Warning Notice: "⚠️ Action Required: Call and verify payment manual".
• Action Buttons for Management:
  - "📞 Call Client": Direct phone dialer.
  - "💬 WhatsApp": Launches pre-composed chat with client name and inquiry ID.
  - "✅ Approve & Allot": Launches Flat Allotment & Advance Verification modal.
  - "❌ Reject Request": Opens rejection modal with mandatory reason logging.

SLIDE 9: Flat/Unit Allotment & Building Lifecycle Progress Tracking
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Goal: Manage construction from day 1 ground-breaking to key handover.
• Stage 1: Approve & Allot Modal:
  - Admin verifies token advance payment received via phone.
  - Assigns specific Flat / Villa Number (e.g., "Villa #14 - Phase 1" or "Flat 402").
  - Automatically transitions status to "Approved" and stage to "Needs to Start".
• Stage 2: Update Stage & Progress Modal:
  - Dropdown stage selector: "Needs to Start" | "Processing" | "Finished".
  - Interactive % Completion Slider: 0% to 100%.
  - Site Progress Notes Input: Live updates that appear instantly on the client's portal 
    (e.g., "First floor slab curing in progress. Electrical piping laid.").
• Stage 3: Finished / Handover:
  - Automatically locks completion at 100% with green achievement status.

SLIDE 10: Direct Customer Registration (+ Add Customer with Flat No)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Goal: Cater to offline customers, office walk-ins, and direct site bookings.
• Features of "+ Add Customer" Tab:
  - Form fields: Customer Name, Phone, Email, Category, BHK, and Flat/Unit Number.
  - Immediately allots the flat, marks status as "Approved", and injects the customer 
    into the active construction pipeline without requiring them to fill the web form.

SLIDE 11: Multi-Channel Tab Synchronization & Mobile Responsiveness
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Goal: Flawless experience whether viewed on a 4K desktop monitor or a smartphone.
• Responsive Architecture:
  1. Desktop: Header navigation + on-page segmented pill bar + wide metric cards.
  2. Mobile & Tablet:
     - Header navigation elegantly collapses.
     - On-page swipeable/scrollable pill bar allows 1-tap switching between all stages.
     - Metric cards auto-adapt into a clean, readable 2-column mobile grid.
     - Smooth auto-centering (`scrollIntoView`) on pill click.
     - Active state synchronized simultaneously across on-page pills, header tabs, 
       KPI metric cards, and mobile hamburger drawer.
